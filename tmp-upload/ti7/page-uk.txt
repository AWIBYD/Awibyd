BYD Super DM Plug-in Hybrid Cars

More and more drivers are choosing BYD plug-in hybrid vehicles to enjoy the smooth driving experience of electric mobility, with the reassurance of an extended driving range.




Thanks to Super DM-i technology, our vehicles prioritise electric driving: complete most of your journeys in EV mode and use the combustion engine only when needed. This delivers lower fuel consumption, quiet comfort, and a combined driving range of up to 1,505 km (WLTP)*.
