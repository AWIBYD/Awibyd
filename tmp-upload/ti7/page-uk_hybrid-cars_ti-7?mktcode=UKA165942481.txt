SUBMIT

The BYD Ti 7 DM-p is a tech-driven, family-focused urban box SUV designed for modern city life. It combines a dignified, boxy design, a spacious and versatile interior, advanced hybrid performance and intelligent in-car technologies - making it the ideal choice for families who want practical, comfort and innovation in one.

Ti 7 DM-p

Three-part silhouette

A clean, geometric structure defines the BYD Ti 7, with a clearly articulated three-part silhouette that enhances visual balance and presence. The upright body and horizontal lines create a strong, structured form that maximises both spaces and proportion.

Signature LED headlights

A distinctive front lighting signature defines the BYD Ti 7, with a clean, horizontal elements and integrated design. The LED daytime running light creates a sharp and modern expression, enhancing both visibility and recognisability.

Wide-set LED tail lights

The rear features a distinctive LED tail light, with horizontal elements that emphasise width and structure, creating a grounded and confident presence on the road.

Interior
Designed for comfort. Powered by technology

The Ti 7 features a spacious and intelligent cabin designed for modern family life. A clean, horizontal layout enhances the sense of space, while the dashboard features BYD’s signature 15.6-inch central infotainment touchscreen, equipped with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto connectivity. 

Smart, connected cockpit
A large 15.6-inch central display sits at the heart of the cabin, offering intuitive access to key functions. Seamless smartphone connectivity, wireless charging and a fully integrated DiLink ecosystem create a smooth and connectied driving experience.

Spacious and flexible interior
Generous space throughout the cabin ensures comfort for every passenger, while flexible seating layout adapts easily to both daily use and longer family journeys.

V2L

The BYD Ti 7 is ready to support your outdoor lifestyle with Vehicle to Load (V2L) technology. This turns your car into a power bank, allowing you to easily plug in everything from a coffee machine to lights or video games: the only limit is your own imagination.
